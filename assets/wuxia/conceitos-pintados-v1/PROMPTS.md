# Conceitos elementais — ícones pintados

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. Originais PNG preservados em `originais/`; versões WebP de 256 × 256 usadas na ficha.

## Direção visual

Ícones circulares sobre tinta escura, pintura fosca com pinceladas largas, poucos detalhes e um motivo central. Fundo exterior transparente. Cores contidas, sem roxo, partículas, aura ou brilho intenso. Sem texto.

## Motivos por elemento

- **Madeira**: one curved woody branch with three broad leaves, sage and forest green.
- **Fogo**: one compact upright flame with three broad tongues, muted burnt orange and ochre.
- **Terra**: three simple layered stones forming a low rocky mountain silhouette, earthy umber and ochre.
- **Metal**: one small angular metal ingot, muted steel grey with one broad pale painted edge.
- **Água**: one curling wave and a single droplet, restrained teal blue.
- **Relâmpago**: one thick angular lightning bolt, muted antique yellow and grey, no electric arcs.
- **Vento**: three sweeping curved wind strokes, subdued sage grey and pale grey, no debris.
- **Gelo**: three simple angular ice crystals, cool desaturated blue grey, matte and opaque, no glitter.
- **Ilusão**: one stylized half-closed eye painted with a slight offset echo brushstroke, muted sage grey and antique bronze.
- **Luz**: one small sun disk with four short broad rays, muted pale ochre gold, matte paint without any glow.
- **Escuridão / Vazio**: one empty dark central opening surrounded by two or three broken crescent brushstrokes, dark petroleum teal and muted grey, no galaxy, stars or light effects.
- **Tempo**: one very simple hourglass with two broad glass shapes and ochre sand, muted wood brown and stone grey, no numbers.
- **Espaço**: one muted jade sphere surrounded by two broad elliptical painted rings crossing on different axes, desaturated jade grey and antique bronze, like a simple ancient armillary sphere.
